Futomaki Sushi

Thick rolls with shrimp, crab stick, cucumber, avocado and tamagoyaki. The rice does most of the work, so cook it first and let it cool properly.

Photo: Geoff Peters, CC BY 2.0.

  • Prep25 mins
  • Cook20 mins
  • Total45 mins
  • Makes4 rolls

Ingredients

Directions

Serve with

Pickled ginger, wasabi and soy sauce for dipping. Miso soup and edamame turn it into a full meal.

Storing

Keep leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 24 hours. The rice hardens, so it is much better on the day.

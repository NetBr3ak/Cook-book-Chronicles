Zuppa Toscana

A cream soup with Italian sausage, bacon, potato and spinach. It reheats well, so it is worth making the whole pot.

Photo: Joy, CC BY 2.0, cropped.

  • Prep20 mins
  • Cook55 mins
  • Total1 hr 15 mins
  • Servings6

Ingredients

Directions

Serve with

Crusty bread for the broth, and a sharp green salad to cut the cream.

Storing

Cool it first, then keep it in a shallow airtight container in the fridge for up to three days. Reheat it thoroughly on the stove or in the microwave.

Freezing

It freezes for up to three months. Thaw it in the fridge overnight, then reheat it gently but thoroughly on the stove.
